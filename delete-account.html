Account & Data Requests

You can request account deletion or removal of specific personal data at any time. This page explains your options and how to contact us.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, the following data is permanently removed:

  1. Your account — email address, display name (handle), and login credentials
  2. Your messages — all feed messages and direct messages you have sent
  3. Your reactions — all emoji reactions you have added to messages
  4. Your push notification token — your device will no longer receive notifications

Note: Feed messages already expire automatically after 1 hour, and direct messages are deleted after 30 days regardless of account status.

Delete your account

Account deletion permanently removes your account and all associated data as described above. To request deletion:

  1. Email alcoan@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request"
  2. Include the email address or display name (handle) associated with your account
  3. We will confirm deletion within 7 days and send a confirmation email once complete

If you signed in with Google, your Google account itself is not affected — only your Charea account and associated data will be deleted.

Delete specific data without deleting your account

If you want to remove specific personal data but keep your account, you can request that too. Examples include:

  1. Removal of your display name (handle) and replacement with a placeholder
  2. Deletion of your direct message history
  3. Removal of your push notification token
  4. Erasure of your location data from our servers

To request partial data deletion:

  1. Email alcoan@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Include your email address or handle and specify clearly which data you want removed
  3. We will respond within 30 days in accordance with GDPR
