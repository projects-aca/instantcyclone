Delete from the app

You can delete your account directly from within the app — no need to contact us.

1
Open Easy Mealplan and go to the Profile tab.
2
Tap the ··· menu in the top-right corner of your profile card.
3
Tap Delete Account and confirm when prompted.
⚠️  Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Your account and all data are removed immediately.

What gets deleted

Your account profile and login credentials
All recipes you created
All week plans and shopping lists
Households you own (if you are the sole member)
Any pending invitations

Need help?

If you cannot access the app, contact us by email and we will handle the request manually within 30 days.
