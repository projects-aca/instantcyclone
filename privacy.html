Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Introduction

Meal Plan is a personal meal planning app. This policy explains what data we collect and how we use it.

Data We Collect

We collect the following data when you use the app: • Account info: your email address and display name when you sign in. • Recipes: names, ingredients, instructions, and photos you add. • Meal plans: your weekly schedule. • Shopping list: items and check states. • Household data: shared between members you invite. • App settings: language, units, and preferences stored locally on your device.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the app's features — syncing your recipes and plans across devices and sharing within your household. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

We use Firebase (by Google) for authentication and cloud storage. Your data is stored on Google's servers subject to Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Data Retention

Your data is stored until you delete your account or remove data manually. Household data is retained until the household is dissolved.

Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below.
