Privacy Policy for Charea

Last updated: June 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Charea, operated by Instant Cyclone, collects, uses, and handles your information when you use the Charea mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

1. Who We Are

Charea is operated by:

Instant Cyclone

Denmark

Contact: alcoan@gmail.com

As a service directed at users in the European Union, we are committed to compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information Collection

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: email address and password when you register, or authentication data if you sign in with Google.
  • Display name (handle): the name you choose when you create your account. This is permanent and visible to other users.
  • Messages: the content of messages you post in the feed or send via private direct messages.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Approximate location: your device's GPS coordinates, collected only while the app is in use (foreground only). We use this solely to show you messages from nearby users and to deliver your messages to users in your area. Your precise location is never stored or shared with other users.
  • Device information: platform type (iOS, Android, web) for push notification delivery.
  • Push notification token: a device token used to send you notifications. This is stored on our servers and linked to your account.

2.3 Cookie and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, Instant Cyclone will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Show you messages posted by users near your location
  • Deliver your messages to nearby users
  • Send you push notifications (replies, mentions, direct messages, or nearby activity based on your preferences)
  • Enforce our community guidelines and respond to reports of abuse
  • Maintain the security and integrity of the Service

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or sale to third parties.

4. Data Retention

  • Feed messages expire and are permanently deleted after 1 hour.
  • Direct messages are permanently deleted after 30 days.
  • Reported content may be retained for longer while under moderation review.
  • Account data (handle, email) is retained until you delete your account.
  • Location data is used in real time and is not stored beyond what is needed to deliver the Service.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties except:

  • Supabase: authentication and database hosting (EU data processing agreement in place).
  • Railway: backend server hosting.
  • Expo / Google Firebase: push notification delivery.
  • Legal obligations: if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

All third-party processors are bound by data processing agreements and may not use your data for their own purposes.

6. Your Rights Under GDPR

As a user in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Right of access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure: request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to restriction: request that we limit processing of your data.
  • Right to data portability: receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: object to processing of your personal data.
  • Right to withdraw consent: where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at alcoan@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance: processing necessary to provide the Service you signed up for (location, messages, account data).
  • Legitimate interests: security monitoring, abuse prevention, and service improvement.
  • Legal obligation: retaining data required by law.

8. Children

Charea is not directed at children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at alcoan@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/WSS), hashed authentication credentials via Supabase, and access controls on our backend systems. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Where processing is based on consent, you provide that consent by affirmatively opting in to the relevant feature or action. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal. Processing based on other lawful bases is carried out as described above.
